Privacy Policy

Bank Statement to Tally (the “App”), operated by Ajai R Krishnan. Contact: ajai.rk8@gmail.com. Last updated: 5 November 2026.

1. Summary

The App runs in your browser or desktop software. Bank statements are read and processed on your device only. We have no server and do not collect, store or sell your data. Signing in with Google is optional.

2. Data processed on your device

  • Bank statement files you open are parsed locally and are never uploaded by the App.
  • Client profiles, rules and settings are saved in your browser’s local storage on your device.

3. Optional Google sign-in and Google Drive backup

If you choose “Sign in with Google”, the App requests these permissions:

  • openid and email: to show the Google account you signed in with.
  • drive.appdata: to save and read one backup file (client_profiles_backup.json) in a hidden app-specific folder of your own Google Drive. The App cannot see any other file in your Drive.

The backup contains your client profiles, rules and settings (which may include client names and ledger names). It does not contain bank statements. Backup and restore happen only when you click the buttons. Data goes directly between your device and your Google Drive; it does not pass through any server of ours. Access tokens are kept in memory only for the current session. Your email address is stored in your browser’s local storage to remember that you signed in.

The App’s use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

4. Feedback form

If you submit the feedback form, the text you type is sent to the form service Web3Forms (api.web3forms.com) and delivered to the operator. Do not include confidential client data in feedback.

5. Sharing

We do not sell, share or transfer your data to third parties, except to Google (Drive backup, only if you use it) and Web3Forms (feedback, only if you submit it).

6. Your control

  • You can use the App without signing in.
  • Click “Sign out” in the App, or remove access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Removing access also lets you delete the app’s hidden Drive data from Google Drive settings.
  • Clear your browser’s site data to delete local profiles and settings.

7. Security and children

Data stays on your device or in your own Google account, protected by their security. The App is intended for professional use and is not directed at children.

8. Changes

Changes to this policy will be posted on this page with a new date.
